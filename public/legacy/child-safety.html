Vybein Child Safety Standards

Vybein is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE/CSAM) on our platform. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward any content or behavior that harms minors.

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

Vybein strictly prohibits:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Sexual exploitation of minors
  • Grooming behavior
  • Any inappropriate interaction involving individuals under 18 years of age

Any user found engaging in such activities will be permanently banned and reported to the appropriate authorities.

2. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report child safety concerns by:

  • Using the in-app reporting feature
  • Emailing us directly at: trustocare@gmail.com

All reports are reviewed promptly, typically within 24 hours.

3. Content Moderation & Enforcement

Vybein actively monitors content and user activity to detect violations of our child safety standards.

If a violation is identified:

  • The content will be immediately removed
  • The account will be suspended or permanently banned
  • Relevant data may be preserved
  • The case may be reported to national or regional law enforcement authorities, as required by law

4. Compliance with Laws

Vybein complies with all applicable child protection laws and regulations. We cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies and child protection organizations in investigations related to child safety.
