Privacy Policy

VYBEIN ("we", "VYBEIN", "Application") is committed to protecting the privacy of all users of its application. The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to clearly explain how VYBEIN collects, uses, and protects information obtained from its users (whether registered or unregistered). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand how the information provided by you to VYBEIN will be used. This Privacy Policy is published in accordance with: The Information Technology Act, 2000; The Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2011; The Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

User’s Consent

This Privacy Policy may be amended or updated from time to time. By using the VYBEIN Application, the user acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agreed to all the terms of this Privacy Policy. If the user does not agree with this Privacy Policy, they should not use the VYBEIN Application. Continued use of the Application confirms that the user has provided their full and unconditional consent, including consent for the collection, storage, use, processing, and disclosure of their personal information. This Privacy Policy shall be read together with VYBEIN’s applicable Terms of Use / Terms & Conditions.

Collection of Personal Information

  • Mandatory Information: Mobile number (for OTP verification)
  • Other Profile Information: Email ID, Profile image, First name, Last name, Age, Gender, Relationship status, Interests, Description, Hobbies, Location, Microphone access (only for feature usage)

VYBEIN collects only such information that is lawful, necessary, and required for the operation of the platform.

Purpose and Use of Data / Information Collection

  • To verify the identity of the user
  • To provide and manage application services
  • To offer technical support and customer assistance
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the platform
  • To comply with any applicable law, regulation, or judicial order

VYBEIN does not use users’ personal information for any form of unlawful profiling or marketing without consent.

Data Deletion

If a user wishes to delete their complete VYBEIN profile, including all associated personal information, they may use the “Delete Account / Request Account Deletion” option available in the application menu. After 30 days from the submission of the deletion request, the user’s profile and associated data will be automatically and permanently deleted. Additionally, users may contact VYBEIN via email to request data or account deletion: support@vybein.com

After completion of the deletion process, the user’s profile and all associated personal information will be permanently deleted. No personal data will be retained, except information required by law or mandatory legal obligations. VYBEIN provides digital services only within India, and all data deletion requests are handled in accordance with Indian laws and applicable data protection regulations.

Microphone Permission

The VYBEIN application may offer audio-based features to enable smoother, more natural interaction between users, allowing communication through the device’s microphone. This feature is provided solely to enhance usability and user experience. VYBEIN does not access the microphone without explicit user consent, does not perform secret, background, or continuous recording, and activates microphone access only when the user is actively using the related feature.

In-App Chat Rooms & Call Features

Users may create in-app chat rooms and may be provided with a calling option within the application. Calls and chats can take place without exchanging personal mobile numbers. VYBEIN does not store chat messages, voice calls, or voice recordings. Copying, recording, sharing, or redistributing user chat content, profile information, or voice interactions is strictly prohibited.

Log Files, IP Address and Cookies

Vybein may collect certain technical information to improve user experience and ensure platform security. This information may include cookies, log files, IP addresses, and session data. Cookies are small data files stored on the user’s device or browser, which help in collecting information such as user identification, maintaining login sessions, date and time of visits, pages viewed, duration of application usage, registration and verification details. Vybein uses cookies strictly for improving platform performance, enhancing and personalizing user experience, and security and fraud prevention. Vybein does not engage in any aggressive, unauthorized, or external advertising tracking without the user’s explicit consent.
